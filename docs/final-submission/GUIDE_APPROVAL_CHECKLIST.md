# BruteShield — Faculty Guide Approval & Examination Clearance Checklist

## Institutional Clearance Mandate

According to the academic guidelines of **ABES Engineering College, Ghaziabad** (affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. This document serves as the master clearance checklist for the internal supervisor to assess readiness across engineering, documentation, integrity, and presentation dimensions.
