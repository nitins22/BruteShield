# BruteShield — Research Paper Plagiarism & AI Report Insertion Guide

## Academic Context & Mandatory Requirements

In accordance with institutional research ethics standards and university B.Tech capstone policies, the **BruteShield Academic Research Paper Draft** (`BRUTESHIELD_RESEARCH_PAPER.docx` / `.pdf`) must be scanned independently through institutional plagiarism software before submission or faculty review.

1. **Similarity Index Limit:** Strictly **less than 10% (< 10%)**
2. **AI-Generated Content Limit:** Strictly **less than 20% (< 20%)**
3. **Draft Status Disclosure:** Must retain prominent banner: *"Research Paper Draft — Not Yet Published or Peer Reviewed"*
4. **Appendices Attachment:**
   - First page of the official Similarity Report as **Appendix A**.
   - First page of the official AI Content Detection Report as **Appendix B**.
5. **Supervisor Endorsement:** The research paper must be evaluated and signed by the faculty guide using `GUIDE_EVALUATION_RESEARCH_PAPER.pdf` (or Appendix C). Without this signature, the paper cannot be submitted to conferences or journals.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions for Students

### Step 1: Institutional Plagiarism Screening
1. Export or copy `BRUTESHIELD_RESEARCH_PAPER.pdf`.
2. Submit the file to Turnitin / iThenticate under your department's research filter.
3. Configure screening settings:
   - Exclude References / Bibliography: **YES**
   - Exclude Mathematical Formulae / Equation Blocks: **YES**
   - Exclude Standard Methodology Phrases: **YES (1% match threshold)**

### Step 2: Extracting First Pages
1. Download the generated official Similarity Report and AI Detection Report from the portal.
2. Extract Page 1 of each report:
   - `paper_similarity_first_page.pdf`
   - `paper_ai_detection_first_page.pdf`

### Step 3: Assembling the Final Submission File
1. Open `BRUTESHIELD_RESEARCH_PAPER.pdf`.
2. Replace or append behind **Appendix A** the extracted `paper_similarity_first_page.pdf`.
3. Replace or append behind **Appendix B** the extracted `paper_ai_detection_first_page.pdf`.
4. Ensure Appendix C (Supervisor Evaluation Rubric) is completed and physically signed by your faculty guide.
5. Save the compiled packet in `docs/final-submission/research-paper/`.
