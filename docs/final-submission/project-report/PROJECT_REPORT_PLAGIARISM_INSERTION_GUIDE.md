# BruteShield — Project Report Plagiarism & AI Report Insertion Guide

## Academic Context & Mandatory Requirements

In accordance with the regulatory standards of **Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU)** and **ABES Engineering College, Ghaziabad**, every B.Tech Capstone Project Report must satisfy two mandatory computerized integrity audits prior to final spiral binding and oral examination (viva voce):

1. **Similarity / Plagiarism Threshold:** Strictly **less than 10% (< 10%)**
2. **AI-Generated Content Threshold:** Strictly **less than 20% (< 20%)**
3. **First Page Attachment Requirement:**
   - The first page of the official Similarity Report must be bound as **Appendix A**.
   - The first page of the official AI Plagiarism Report must be bound as **Appendix B**.
4. **Faculty Endorsement:** Without the internal guide's verified evaluation and signature (Appendix C), the candidate **cannot** be scheduled for the university viva voce examination.

## Step-by-Step Instructions for Students

### Step 1: Institutional Software Submission
1. Export or copy your finalized `BRUTESHIELD_FINAL_PROJECT_REPORT.docx` or `BRUTESHIELD_FINAL_PROJECT_REPORT.pdf`.
2. Submit the document to your departmental plagiarism administrator or upload it via your student Turnitin / iThenticate / DrillBit institutional account.
3. **Settings configuration for screening:**
   - Exclude Quotes: **ON**
   - Exclude Bibliography / References: **ON**
   - Exclude Small Matches: **1% or 5 words** (per institutional policy)
   - Filter Source Code: Standard programming syntax exclusions active.

### Step 2: Verification of Results
1. Ensure the **Overall Similarity Index** is **< 10%**.
   - If any section flags common engineering definitions, rephrase in your own authentic undergraduate voice.
2. Ensure the **AI Writing / AI Content Percentage** is **< 20%**.
   - If AI detection exceeds threshold, review sections using `STUDENT_AUTHORSHIP_REVIEW.md`.

### Step 3: Extracting the Official First Page
1. Download the full digital receipt/report from Turnitin or DrillBit.
2. Open the PDF in Adobe Acrobat, PDF-XChange, or standard browser print dialog.
3. Extract **Page 1** (the summary page containing the document title, author name, date, word count, barcode/digital identifier, and colored percentage metric bar).
4. Save as: `official_similarity_report_page1.pdf` and `official_ai_report_page1.pdf`.

### Step 4: Binding into Final Report
1. Open `BRUTESHIELD_FINAL_PROJECT_REPORT.pdf`.
2. Locate **Appendix A** (Page 30/31).
3. Insert or replace the placeholder page with your extracted `official_similarity_report_page1.pdf`.
4. Locate **Appendix B** (Page 31/32).
5. Insert or replace the placeholder page with your extracted `official_ai_report_page1.pdf`.
6. Verify that **Appendix C** (Guide Evaluation Form) is printed for physical signature by your internal guide, project coordinator, and HOD.
7. Proceed to commercial print shop for **A4 spiral binding** (1.25" inner margin preserved).
